The wallet coin icons are scaled copies of these project graphics:

- Bitcoin: https://bitcoin.design/assets/images/guide/getting-started/visual-language/bitcoin-symbol.png
- Zcash: https://zfnd.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Logo-Color.webp
- Bitcoin Cash: https://bitcoincash.org/img/green/bitcoin-cash-circle.svg

The Dinero coin artwork is maintained as `resources/branding/dinero-coin.svg`.
Run `scripts/render-dinero-icon.sh` from the repository to regenerate its
transparent PNG, `.icns`, and `.ico` assets. The Wallet and Portfolio use
`resources/DIN.iconset/icon_32x32@2x.png`; the same design is used for the
app icon, Swap form, and QR logo.
