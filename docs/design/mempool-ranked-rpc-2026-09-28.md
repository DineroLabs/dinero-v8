# Captured ranked mempool RPC entries

Actual mempool.getbyfee acquires the service pool owner and captures immutable actual-family bodies together with their matching entry fee and fee_rate under one pool read lock. It constructs the response from that capture. Existing package-selection index ordering is preserved; its score is not necessarily the per-transaction fee_rate reported in the response. Missing, empty, wrongly identified or duplicate indexed entries in the requested prefix refuse the read before a transaction prefix is published. Historical compatibility explicitly unwraps every captured entry and refuses unsupported families.

The capture is bounded by the caller's existing count and available index. It is not a whole-pool completeness certificate, reservation, admission permission or continuing membership. Parameter defaults, ordering and transaction policy are unchanged. Orchard structural fixtures exercise the reader only; actual Orchard typed admission remains unavailable.
