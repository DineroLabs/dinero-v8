## Scope and remaining work

Eligibility holds as of the durable attempt commit. A later reorganization may occur before wallet signing, admission or settlement. This change does not make chain selection and payment dispatch one atomic operation, certify lasting permission, or resolve source-block orphan debt and liability policy. It does not prove the funding wallet owns the coinbase, authenticate worker/job contributions or a complete pool catalog, detect deleted allocations or backup rollback, or independently revalidate historical proof of work/fees. Stored confirmations remain an additional conservative eligibility check but cannot substitute for actual canonical depth. Mainnet remains unset; the production notification provider and release gates remain open.
