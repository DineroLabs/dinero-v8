# Owned mempool callbacks after publication

The acceptance path captures the configured accepted and relay callbacks while holding the pool mutex, before publishing an entry. It completes size-based cleanup under that same mutex, releases the mutex and selected-chain read guard, then calls the captured callbacks. Callback replacement cannot destroy an in-flight capture. Setter swaps release the prior captures outside the mutex. Explicit broadcast similarly copies its callback under a shared lock and invokes it afterward.

Notifications describe an admission that occurred; they do not promise continued membership after eviction or other changes. Callback exceptions propagate after publication. A wallet must retain the signed body and reservations when a submission outcome is ambiguous. This change does not establish transactional admission, durable notification delivery, automatic retry or acknowledgment by every consumer.
